Who's behind it

Built by working musicians, for working musicians.

I'm Michelle, a concert pianist. I trained at the highest level, won the Prix d'Europe, and I perform across Canada and Europe. I also spent years lecturing on entrepreneurship for classical musicians at conservatories like Maastricht and Codarts, because I learned the hard way that talent alone does not build a career.

In 2020 my fellow musician Deanna Petre and I started The Fearless Artist Mastermind. Since then we've coached hundreds of musicians around the world to stop waiting to be picked and start building careers that actually pay.

We are not marketers who wandered into music. We are working artists who figured out the business side and put it into a system, so you do not have to learn it alone the way we did.
